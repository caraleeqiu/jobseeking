About Acme AI
We're a Series A startup (raised $18M) building AI agents for SMB operations.
~25 people, fully remote across US and LatAm. Backed by top-tier VCs.

The Role — Founding Marketer
You'll be our first marketing hire, owning growth end to end: positioning,
demand gen, content, and community. You'll work directly with the founders.

What you'll do
- Build our go-to-market motion from scratch (0 to 1)
- Own paid + organic acquisition and report on CAC/LTV
- Launch our LatAm expansion (Spanish-speaking markets)
- Set up marketing automation and experimentation

What we're looking for
- 6+ years in growth/marketing, ideally at startups
- Proven 0-to-1 experience with real numbers
- Comfortable with data and modern AI tooling
- Bonus: LatAm market experience, Spanish
